# Growth Readiness Scorecard — Promo Video Script

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 (Reels / TikTok / Shorts / Stories) · **Length:** ~45–55 seconds
**Style:** Talking head to camera, with screen recordings of the scorecard cut in. On-screen captions throughout (most people watch with the sound off).

---

## HOOK (0–4s)

**ON CAMERA** (close framing, direct eye contact, no intro)
> "If your ads aren't working, more budget won't fix it. It'll just lose money faster."

**TEXT ON SCREEN:** More ad spend ≠ more customers

---

## PROBLEM (4–15s)

**ON CAMERA**
> "Most businesses I talk to don't have a traffic problem. They have a *leak*, somewhere between the first click and the sale."

**B-ROLL:** Quick cuts of an ads dashboard with money going out, an inbox with no replies, a phone with no new enquiries.

> "Maybe the offer isn't clear. Maybe leads never get followed up. Maybe you can't even tell which ad brought in the sale. And until you know *which* one it is, you're guessing."

**TEXT ON SCREEN:** Offer? Message? Funnel? Tracking? 🤷🏾

---

## SOLUTION (15–35s)

**ON CAMERA**
> "So I built a free tool to find the leak. It's called the Growth Readiness Scorecard."

**SCREEN RECORDING:** The scorecard landing page, then click "Start My Free Scorecard".

> "You answer 30 quick multiple-choice questions. It takes about five minutes."

**SCREEN RECORDING:** Scroll through 2–3 questions, tapping answers quickly.

> "It checks six areas: your offer, your audience, your message, your funnel, whether you're ready for paid ads, and your tracking."

**TEXT ON SCREEN** (one line at a time, quickly):
Offer Strength · Audience Clarity · Message Clarity · Funnel Readiness · Paid Media Readiness · Tracking & Measurement

> "Then you get a score out of 100 straight away, your two biggest gaps, and one specific thing to fix next in each area."

**SCREEN RECORDING:** The results page: score counting up, category bars filling in, an insight card.

---

## REFRAME (35–43s)

**ON CAMERA**
> "If you score low, that's good news. It means you found the problem *before* you spent more money on it. If you score high, you'll know exactly where your next 20% is coming from."

**TEXT ON SCREEN:** Low score = money saved. High score = room to scale.

---

## CTA (43–52s)

**ON CAMERA**
> "It's completely free. No card, no catch. Take it now at thedamlekdigital.com/scorecard, or tap the link in my bio. Then comment your score below. I want to see where everyone lands."

**TEXT ON SCREEN:** 🔗 thedamlekdigital.com/scorecard · Comment your score 👇

**END CARD (2s):** thedamlek digital logo + "Free Growth Readiness Scorecard · 5 min"

---

## Alternate hooks (to A/B test)

1. "Answer this honestly: if I gave you ₦1 million for ads tomorrow, would your business actually make it back?"
2. "Five minutes. Thirty questions. One number that tells you if you're ready to run ads."
3. "I can tell whether your ads will work before you spend a naira. Here's how."
4. "Your ads might not be the problem. Here's how to find out what is."

## Production notes

- Record the hook in 3–4 different takes and energy levels. The hook decides whether anyone watches the rest.
- Keep each cut under 3 seconds and don't hold on a talking-head shot for more than about 5 seconds before cutting to the screen.
- Record the scorecard on your phone (portrait) so screen captures fill the frame.
- "Comment your score" drives engagement, and it gives you warm leads to DM.
- Cut 15s version: Hook → "So I built a free tool…" → results-page screen recording → CTA.
