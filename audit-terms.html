01What's included

The 7-Day Ad Profit Leak Audit ("the audit") is a review of your live Meta ad account, your conversion tracking and your email follow-up, done by Damilola Adeleke, trading as thedamlek digital ("I", "me"). You receive:

  • A written leak report, with each finding estimated in dollars per month.
  • A 90-day fix roadmap, ranked by revenue impact.
  • A 60-minute walkthrough call to go through the findings.

The audit is analysis and recommendations. It doesn't include making changes in your account, building campaigns, writing emails or producing creative. That work is part of the retainer.

02Price and payment

The audit costs $297 (USD), paid in full upfront through Stripe. It's a one-time payment with no subscription and nothing else to buy.

03Timeline

Your report is delivered within 7 days of the day I have both view access to your accounts and your completed intake form. I'll email you to confirm that start date. The walkthrough call happens on or after day 7, at a time you book.

If I haven't received access within 30 days of purchase, I'll check in with you. If you no longer want the audit at that point, you'll get a full refund.

04What I need from you

  • View access to your Meta ad account, and to your pixel or dataset in Events Manager.
  • Read-only access to your email platform, if you have one.
  • The completed intake form.
  • Reasonably prompt answers if I have a question during the 7 days.

If access is removed or reduced during the audit, the 7-day clock pauses until it's restored.

05The $1,000 guarantee

If the leak report doesn't identify at least $1,000 per month in wasted or recoverable ad spend, you can ask for a full refund of the $297.

What counts. "Wasted spend" is money going to ads, audiences, placements or campaigns that the account's own data shows aren't paying back, or are paying back far worse than the rest of the account. "Recoverable spend" is value you're already paying for but losing, for example conversions that happen but aren't reported to Meta, or buyers and leads who get no email follow-up. Each finding in the report shows its monthly dollar estimate and how I worked it out, based on your account's recent data.

Who it covers. The guarantee applies when, at the start of the audit:

  • the account has spent an average of $5,000 or more per month on Meta over the previous 30 days, and
  • the account has at least 30 days of campaign history, and
  • I have the view access listed in section 4 for the whole audit.

Accounts below these thresholds can still buy the audit, but the guarantee doesn't apply. If I see from your intake form that your account isn't eligible, I'll tell you before I start and offer you a full refund instead, so you can decide.

06How to claim a refund

Email damilola@thedamlekdigital.com within 14 days of receiving your leak report. I'll confirm eligibility and refund the full $297 to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank. You keep the report and roadmap.

07Cancellations and other refunds

  • Before I start: you can cancel for a full refund at any time before your confirmed start date.
  • After I start: refunds are available only under the guarantee in section 5.
  • If I can't deliver: if for any reason I can't complete the audit, you get a full refund.

If you start the Meta + Email Growth Retainer within 14 days of your walkthrough call, the $297 is credited against your first month. The retainer is $997/month with a 3-month minimum, then month to month, and is by application. Buying the audit doesn't commit you to the retainer, and the retainer has its own agreement. If you received a guarantee refund, there's no credit to apply.

  • When the audit is finished you can remove my access at any time, and I'll remind you to.

10Results and liability

The dollar figures in the report are estimates based on your account's data at the time of the audit. They aren't a promise of future results, which depend on how and when fixes are carried out, your offer, your market and Meta's platform. My total liability for the audit is limited to the $297 you paid.
