FIG. 02 — WHAT A TEARDOWN LOOKS FOR

Most leaks are in the same six places.

  1. 01

    Creative fatigue

    Ads that have run so long the audience scrolls past them.

  2. 02

    Weak hooks

    First lines that talk about the brand instead of the buyer's problem.

  3. 03

    Offer–page mismatch

    The ad promises one thing and the landing page says another.

  4. 04

    Wrong audience, same message

    One ad trying to convert buyers who need different reasons.

  5. 05

    Tracking gaps

    Conversions that happen but never reach Meta, so budget goes to the wrong ads.

  6. 06

    No follow-up

    Leads and first-time buyers who never get an email after the click.

The free teardown covers what's visible from outside: your ads, offers and landing pages. The paid audit goes inside the account.
